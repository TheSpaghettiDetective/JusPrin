> What other chat apps do
> Open-source chat apps keep one neutral hint all the way through. I checked their source code. Open WebUI always shows “Send a Message”, and LibreChat always shows “Message {model}”.

> Claude and ChatGPT (from memory, not checked). I believe Claude.ai shows “How can I help you today?” in a new chat and “Reply to Claude…” afterwards, and ChatGPT shows “Ask anything” throughout. The browser was signed out of both, and web searches didn’t confirm the exact text.
> General form-design guidance: a placeholder disappears as soon as you type, so it should never carry anything you need to remember. Some people also mistake hint text for something already filled in. That argues for short hints, especially after the first message.
