The slice finish is already event-driven, right?
Half of it is. The detection is event-driven; the finishing of the tool call is not.
Event-driven part. The adapter listens to Orca’s slice-status event and publishes a workspace change when a plate starts or stops being sliced. The coordinator subscribes to those changes and, in the subscription itself, keeps the two flags for the slice it started: `m_slice_seen_running` and `m_slice_ended` (ToolExecutionCoordinator.cpp:377). Polled part. The waiting `slice_start` call is not finished inside that subscription. It is finished in `finish_slice_waits`, which runs from `pump()`, which `AgentHost::pump_tools` calls on the app’s periodic tick (AgentHost.cpp:1908). 2. The refusal. Same moment, same check, once the adapter records the ValidateError into the snapshot. Today the poll can’t see it at all and runs 15 minutes.
3. A run that never reports. The 15-minute cap. That is a timeout, and a timeout wants one timer, not a check on every tick.
