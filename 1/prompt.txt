- A plate has only a few settings of its own (bed type, print sequence, first-layer filament order, spiral vase; src/slic3r/GUI/PlateSettingsDialog.cpp).

**How scope works**

## 3. The bridge (page-initiated requests)

**Transport**
- The page posts with `window.wx.postMessage(JSON)` (`WebShared/bridgeClient.ts:58-63`, `post()` at `:149-159`). Page ids are `w-N`.
- The host delivers back through `window.__jusprinBridge.deliver(...)` (`Agent/AgentWebView.cpp:167-174`).
- Incoming messages go `on_script_message` → `AgentHost::on_page_message` (`AgentWebView.cpp:203`, `AgentHost.cpp:975`) → `dispatch_page_message` (`AgentHost.cpp:998-1093`). That function is an if/else chain on `type`.
- Anything the chain doesn't know falls to `m_page_message_handler(type, payload)` (`:1091`). If that returns false, the host sends `bridge_error` `unknown_type`.
