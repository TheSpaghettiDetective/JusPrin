**(b) Plate switching.** Snapshots are taken twice:
- `Plater::select_plate` takes `take_snapshot("select partplate!")` every time, even for the same plate (Plater.cpp:17668).
- `PartPlateList::select_plate` takes another only if the index changed and the list is initialized (PartPlate.cpp:4781-4784).
