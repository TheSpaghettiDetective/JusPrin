The five roles carry weight, and the guard you specified forbids `font-weight`, so **"bold 14" and "regular 12" cannot exist any more**. I mapped by function rather than by size:
- Lost bold (now `body`): `.current-setup-title` (Figma said "Body Bold"), `.tool-title` (rule deleted), `button.primary`, `button.link`, `.context-summary .project`. `&lt;strong&gt;` elements in `.chat-list-row-top` and `.configure-agent` keep UA bold by inheriting body instead of declaring a role.
