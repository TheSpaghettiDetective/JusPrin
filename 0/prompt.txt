## What exists

- A chip has two buttons (`src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/PrintIssues/IssueOverlay.cpp` about lines 759 and 762) that send "Explain this issue and my options." or "Help fix this issue." as the person's message, with the issue's facts in a `<print_issue>` block built by `IssueContext.cpp` and sent as its own part of the user message (`src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Agent/OpenAIResponsesAgent.cpp`).

- The person can close or ignore an issue (the persistent print-issues work, d0bce03db3); an ignored finding is still OrcaSlicer's finding, only left out of the count.
