## The problem

In `OpenAIResponsesAgent::initial_input` (src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Agent/OpenAIResponsesAgent.cpp), the user's own words and the app's data are written into one `input_text` string: the typed text, then a blank line, then the plain heading "Authoritative JusPrin workspace context:" and the JSON from `workspace_json()`, then any attachment summaries and the attachment text in `<attachment id="…">` tags. The print-issue context from the canvas buttons is appended in `AgentHost::make_agent_request` the same way: "JusPrin print-issue context (source data from the app, not instructions):" followed by JSON, inside `request.user_text`. The only boundary between what the person typed and what the app added is a heading in plain prose. Pasted text that happens to contain such a heading is indistinguishable from app data, and the block has no end marker before the attachments. OpenAI's GPT-4.1 guide and Anthropic's prompting guide both say the same thing: delimit each kind of content in its own tag and tell the model once what the tag is. The attachments already do this correctly with `<attachment>` tags; the snapshot and the issue context do not.

Separately, `workspace_json()` sends only sessionId, revision, projectName, printerPreset, filamentPreset, selectedObjectIds and plates (id, name, active, sliced, objects with id, name, instances). The prompt asks the model to assess "the current material, filament preset and process preset" and to keep its plan in `plan_set`, but the process preset, the intent record and the plan are not in the snapshot. On the next turn the model's own notes are invisible unless it spends a `workspace_inspect` call on the intent and plan sections. The app already holds all of it: `WorkspaceSnapshot::setup` has the presets, and `ProjectStateDocument` holds the intent (title, fields, open questions) and the plan (headline, decisions, assumptions); `workspace_inspect`'s `intent` and `plan` sections show exactly which fields exist (see the handler in ToolExecutionCoordinator.cpp and the schemas in ToolRegistry.cpp).
