## The problem

Kenneth's most important behaviour: as soon as the chat knows what a print is for, it checks whether the selected filament and process presets suit it, and says so. Today that is asked for by the Preparing section of the standing prompt (`AgentUI/src/projectInstructions.fixture.txt`, 2,364 bytes), nine procedural sentences read on every turn, including "what infill should I use". And the facts the check needs are not stated at the moment they matter: the intent tool's reply says only that the record was written; nothing says the printer, filament, process and plate in force were chosen before this purpose existed; a later preset change does not say that the purpose it was checked against still stands; after the person restores an older version, the project description carries the plan's headline, decisions and assumptions but not whether the plan is still valid (`src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Agent/OpenAIResponsesAgent.cpp` about lines 39 to 86 build that description; `src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Agent/ProjectPersistence.cpp` about lines 134, 397 and 424 record edits, plan invalidation and restores; `src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Workspace/ProjectAutosave.cpp` about line 1125 records the restore).

## What to build

**A. Facts, stated truthfully.** The intent tool's reply (`ToolExecutionCoordinator.cpp`, the `intent_update` handler about line 1796; `AgentHost.cpp` about line 624 treats it specially) states what changed: the purpose before and after, or that only the title changed, or that the same purpose was recorded again; and the printer preset, filament preset, process preset and active plate type in force at that moment. It is a statement of fact, not a claim such as "not yet checked", which the app cannot know. The project description that rides with every message gains two facts: whether the setup (printer, filament, process or plate type) has changed since the purpose was recorded, derived from the revision numbers or the edit ledger, and the plan's validity and the reason it was invalidated when it was. Parts the app cannot name are left out, never null.

**B. The skill.** `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` with a trigger for this event, opening with "The rules above still apply", holding only what the model lacks: what checking the setup against the purpose means in JusPrin, namely read the effective settings with `settings_get` including object overrides, since a preset name does not prove its values; find compatible presets with `presets_list` and check the plate type against the filament with `printer_setup_preview`; a material and its saved filament preset are separate choices; keep customizations that still fit; when something does not fit, say why and recommend a material, a compatible preset or a focused setting change; when several changes follow, record the plan and its assumptions with `plan_set` first; and when the person restored an older version, the plan may be stale and the setup is to be checked again, not assumed. Do not repeat the standing rules about asking the person or about scope. Keep it short, well under the model-arrived skill's size. Never write "approve", "authorize", "confirm before" or "ask the user then call again"; tool-call approval was removed on 2026-10-07.

**C. When it fires.** On the continuation request carrying the intent tool's reply when the purpose was recorded for the first time or changed (not when only the title changed or the same purpose was repeated); on the continuation request carrying a `printer_setup` reply that changed the printer, filament, process or plate type while a purpose is recorded; on the next request after the person restored an older version while a purpose is recorded. Not on a request that already carries the model-arrived skill (an import), since that skill already asks for the preset check; say in the report how you avoided firing two skills on one request. Fire once per event.

Leave the sentence about `slice_report` and `slice_inspect` to the final prompt edit, which the main session owns, and leave the print-issue bullet alone (skill 3 removes it). Move into tool descriptions the two one-tool rules: `plan_set`'s description (`src/slic3r/GUI/JusPrin/Agent/ToolRegistry.cpp` about line 1020) gains "before applying several changes for one request, so the person sees the plan and its assumptions first"; `presets_list` and `printer_setup` (about lines 1330 and 1055) gain, in the fewest words, that a material and its saved filament preset are separate choices.
