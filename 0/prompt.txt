     - ToolExecutionCoordinator does propose/approve/pump/execute. Proposal-time binding happens in propose (for example resolvedPath, confirmedSetup, titles).
     - The coordinator keeps the executing activity via `m_executing`, and `clear()` keeps it. `forget_if_closed(action_id)` removes a project_open record after it has reported.
     - AgentHost ignores activities whose session is not the current one.
   - **Workspace boundary.** IWorkspace is implemented by OrcaWorkspaceAdapter and by FakeWorkspace (tests/jusprin_support).
   - **ModalAnswers seam.**
     - GUI_Utils.hpp `DPIAware::ShowModal` calls `answer_modal(*this, answer)`.
