# The app itself

Where JusPrin's own preferences are, and the commands that are about the app rather than a project.

Names in bold are the words on screen. On a Mac the menus named here are in the menu bar. On Windows and Linux the **File** menu is the **File** button in the title bar, and the other menus are in the menu beside it.

I cannot change these preferences myself; I give these steps.

## Open Preferences

- On a Mac, choose **Preferences** in the JusPrin menu, or press Cmd+,.
- On Windows and Linux, choose **Preferences** in the menu beside the **File** button, or press Ctrl+,.
- In a project, the "…" button at the right of the header has **Preferences…**. On Home, it is the gear button at the top right.

The window lists its sections at the left: **Account**, **BYOAI**, **Appearance**, **3D view**, **Projects & files** and **Updates**. Account and BYOAI set up AI; the guide's page on AI chats describes them. **More preferences…** at the bottom opens the app's full list of older preferences.

## Language, theme and units

In Preferences, open **Appearance**.

- **Language**: pick one from the list. JusPrin uses it after it restarts, and offers **Restart now** or **Later**. Restart now saves the project first.
- **Theme**: **Light** or **Dark**. The change shows at once.
- **Units**: **Millimetres** or **Inches**.

## Mouse and touchpad in the 3D view

In Preferences, open **3D view**.

- **Controls**: **Mouse** or **Touchpad**, for how dragging and scrolling move the view.
- **Zoom toward the pointer** zooms to where the pointer is, instead of the middle of the view. **Reverse scroll-wheel zoom** turns the wheel's direction around. **Turn freely** lets the view turn past upside down.
- Under **Mouse buttons**, choose what **Left-drag**, **Middle-drag** and **Right-drag** do: **Turn the view**, **Pan** or **Nothing**.

The keys of the 3D view are listed under **Keyboard Shortcuts** in the **Help** menu of classic view. JusPrin's own Help menu does not have that item.

## Show and hide the panels

An open project has a panel at the left, with the plates and the project, and the AI panel at the right.

- On a Mac, the **View** menu has **Show plates and project panel** and **Show AI panel**. While a panel is shown its item reads **Hide plates and project panel** or **Hide AI panel**.
- On Windows and Linux, the **View** menu has **Plates and project panel** and **AI panel**, ticked while each is shown.
- The header has a button for each: at its far left for the plates panel, at its far right for the AI panel.
- **Home** in the **View** menu goes back to Home.

## Classic view

Classic view is the app's original screen, with all print settings in a panel at the side. Choose **Switch to classic view…** in the **View** menu, or in the "…" menu at the right of the header. To return, click **Back to JusPrin** on the bar across the top, or choose **Switch to JusPrin view** in the **View** menu.

## Updates

JusPrin looks for a newer version once a day, on GitHub. When there is one, **Updates** in Preferences is marked with a dot.

1. In Preferences, open **Updates**. **Check now** looks straight away.
2. When it says a newer version is available, click **Download**. The release's page opens in your browser; download and install it from there.
3. **Skip this version** stops it asking about that version. **Release notes** shows what changed.

Untick **New JusPrin versions** to stop the daily check. The printer and filament profiles that come with JusPrin are updated with JusPrin itself.

## Report a problem or get help

- **Report a problem…** in the **Help** menu opens a new issue on JusPrin's GitHub page in your browser, with the app's version and your system already filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it there.
- **JusPrin guide…** in the **Help** menu opens this guide inside the app, with a search box.
- **Ask AI…** in the **Help** menu opens the AI panel.
- **About JusPrin** shows the version, with **Copy version**, and how to get help. It is in the JusPrin menu on a Mac and in the **Help** menu on Windows and Linux.
